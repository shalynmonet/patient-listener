patient-listener

A speech-capture demo that waits for you to actually finish talking.

This browser doesn't support speech recognition. Try Chrome, Edge, or Safari on iPhone/iPad/Mac instead — see the browser table below.
Tap "Start talking" and say something — pause as long as you like.

Why this exists

Browser speech recognition is built for quick commands: say a sentence, pause, and it stops listening — often mid-thought. That's a poor fit for anyone who speaks more slowly, pauses to find a word, or has a condition that puts gaps between words: older adults, people recovering from a stroke, anyone speaking a second language, or anyone just thinking out loud. patient-listener keeps the microphone open through natural pauses and only finishes when you decide — either a configurable silence timeout or an explicit "I'm done" tap — and on iPhone Safari, where results are often never marked "final" at all, it still hands back exactly what was shown on screen instead of silently losing it.
